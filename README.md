# Supply Chain Management Dashboard 📊

## 🔍 About the Project
This project was created as part of my Business Analyst internship.  
It provides a **comprehensive analysis of supply chain performance** using Power BI dashboards.  
The dashboards cover **Inventory, Order Fulfillment, Supplier, Transportation, Costs, and Executive Summary**.


## 📂 Dataset
- Provided by internship company (CSV file)
- Contains supplier, warehouse, inventory, orders, shipping, and cost details
- Columns include: Product, Supplier, Stock Levels, Lead Time, Shipping Times, Costs, Revenue, Profit, etc.


## 📊 Dashboards & KPIs

### 1️⃣ Inventory Management
- **KPIs:** Total Inventory Value, Days of Inventory Remaining, Stockout Products
- **Visuals:** Bar (Inventory by Product), Line (Inventory Trend), Table (Reorder Candidates)
- **Insights:** Identifies stockouts, reorder needs, and inventory carrying risks.


### 2️⃣ Order Fulfillment
- **KPIs:** Total Orders, Orders Fulfilled, On-Time Delivery Rate, Avg Fulfillment Time
- **Visuals:** Funnel (Order Status), Line (On-Time % Trend), Bar (On-Time by Product)
- **Insights:** Measures delivery performance, highlights late orders.


### 3️⃣ Supplier Performance
- **KPIs:** Avg Supplier Lead Time, Avg Defect Rate, Supplier Cost Efficiency
- **Visuals:** Bar (Lead Time by Supplier), Line (Defect Trend), Table (Supplier Summary)
- **Insights:** Evaluates supplier reliability, defect risks, and cost effectiveness.


### 4️⃣ Transportation Efficiency
- **KPIs:** Avg Transit Time, Avg Shipping Cost, On-Time Transport Rate
- **Visuals:** Scatter (Transit vs Cost by Carrier), Bar (On-Time Rate by Carrier), Map (Route Performance)
- **Insights:** Identifies efficient vs costly carriers, route optimization needs.


### 5️⃣ Supply Chain Costs
- **KPIs:** Total Costs, Transportation Costs, Inventory Holding Costs, Order Processing Costs
- **Visuals:** Pie (Cost Distribution), Line (Cost Trend), Stacked Bar (Supplier Costs)
- **Insights:** Highlights cost drivers and opportunities for cost reduction.


### 6️⃣ Executive Dashboard (Summary)
- **KPIs:** Total Revenue, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, On-Time Delivery Rate, Avg Supplier Lead Time, Avg Defect Rate
- **Visuals:** Bar (Supplier Lead Time), Pie (Cost Distribution), Line (Orders Fulfilled Trend), Scatter (Transit vs Cost by Mode)
- **Insights:** Provides leadership with an overall supply chain health snapshot.


## ⚡ Tech Stack
- **Tool:** Power BI
- **Languages:** DAX, Power Query (for cleaning)
- **Data Handling:** CSV dataset
